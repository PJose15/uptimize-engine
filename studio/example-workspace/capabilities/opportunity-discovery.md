# Opportunity and workflow discovery — draft procedure

Input: a business owner's objective, interview/observation evidence, workflow boundaries, available baseline.
Output: process map, evidence-based problem statement, options, cost assumptions and a scoped pilot brief.

Observe the actual work: trigger, participants, steps, systems, handoffs, exceptions and outcome. Record frequency, elapsed/active time, errors, rework and economic effect only where supported. Include opportunities for better decisions, new experiences and new capacity as well as lost revenue. Compare process changes, existing software, deterministic automation and AI. Identify data/access dependencies and who will operate the result.

For the pilot specify one outcome, allowed inputs, users, baseline, success measure, owner, duration and review decision. Mark unknowns as unknown; estimates are assumptions. A recurring problem becomes a product hypothesis only after repeatability and demand are investigated.
